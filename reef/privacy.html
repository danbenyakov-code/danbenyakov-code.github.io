Privacy Policy: Lagoon Isle: Reef Rescue

Last updated: October 30, 2026. Operator: Lagoon Isle. Contact: info.naimly@gmail.com

What we collect

  • Account (optional): if you choose "Continue with Google", we receive your Google user ID, email address and display name through Google Firebase Authentication. You can always play as a guest without an account.
  • Game progress: your level, stars, gems, lives and boosters are stored on your device and, if you signed in, in the cloud (Google Firebase Firestore) so you can continue on another device.
  • Purchases: payments are handled by Google Play. We never receive card details. Purchase data is processed by RevenueCat to validate and deliver purchases.
  • Ads: the game shows rewarded ads through Google AdMob only when you choose to watch one for a reward. AdMob may collect an advertising ID and device information. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

How we use it

To sign you in, save and sync your progress, deliver your purchases, show rewarded ads and keep the game stable. We do not sell personal data.

Third-party services

Google Firebase (sign-in and cloud save), Google AdMob (ads), RevenueCat (purchase validation) and Google Play (payments). Each has its own privacy policy: Google, RevenueCat.

Deleting your account and data

In the game open Settings, then Delete account: your account and cloud progress are deleted immediately. Guest data lives only on your device and is removed when you uninstall the game. You can also email us and we will delete your data within 30 days.

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played and to improve it. It collects an app instance ID, in-game events (such as sessions, progress, sharing and rating prompts), device model, Android version, app version, language and approximate location (country or region, based on IP address). We do not send your name or email address to Analytics. See Firebase privacy information.

Ratings and sharing

The game may ask you to rate it through the Google Play in-app review feature; your rating goes directly to Google Play. If you choose to share, the game opens your device's share menu and nothing is sent unless you pick an app and send it yourself.

Feedback and support form

From the game settings (and occasionally after a good moment in the game) you can send us a message. When you press Send, we receive: the text you wrote, the message type you chose (suggestion, problem, support or other), the email address you typed (optional, only if you want a reply), the game name and version, your phone model and Android version, and how many times you opened the game. The message is delivered to our support inbox through Web3Forms, a form-to-email service. We use it only to answer you and to improve the game, we never sell it, and you can ask us to delete it at any time by writing to info.naimly@gmail.com. If sending fails, the game can open your own email app instead, and nothing is sent until you press send there.

Your rights (EEA, UK and Switzerland: GDPR)

Controller: Lagoon Isle, contact info.naimly@gmail.com.

Legal bases: we process account, cloud save and purchase data to provide the game you asked for (performance of a contract); analytics, security and fraud prevention data on the basis of our legitimate interest in running and improving the game; and personalised advertising only with your consent, which you give or refuse in the consent message shown on first launch.

Your rights: you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, restrict or object to its processing, and withdraw your consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. Email us and we will answer within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

International transfers: our service providers (Google and RevenueCat) may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Retention: cloud data is kept until you delete your account; analytics data is kept according to Google Analytics retention settings; data on your device is removed when you uninstall the game.

We do not sell personal data.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes
