How a level works

Every level gives you a goal and a set number of moves. Swap two neighbouring tiles to make a line of three or more of the same tile. Clear sand and break nets on the board to open a path, then guide the baby turtles, octopuses and dolphins down to the reef floor.

When a level is won, the moves you did not use still count, so finishing early is always good.

Every power-up and how to make it

Wave: match 4 tiles in a row. It clears a full line.

Coco Bomb: make an L or a T shape. It blasts the tiles around it.

Ocean Gem: match 5 in a row. Swap it with any tile to clear every tile of that colour.

Combos: swap two power-ups into each other for a much bigger chain reaction. A Gem plus another power-up is the strongest move in the game.

Tips for hard levels

Look at the bottom of the board first. Matches low on the board make new tiles fall from the top and often start free cascades.

Do not fire a power-up the moment you make it. Save it for the spot where a net or a creature is stuck.

Clear the column under a sea friend first, so it can drop as soon as the way is open.

If you are one move short, a Wave or a Hammer is usually cheaper than replaying the level.

Boosters: Hammer, Shuffle and Wave

Hammer breaks one tile anywhere, ideal for the last net.

Shuffle mixes the board when no good match is left.

Wave clears a line before you even move.

Daily gifts give you free gems and boosters, so it pays to open the game once a day.

Reef Buddy, streaks and rewards

A sea friend you rescued swims with you as your Reef Buddy and clears tiles on its own. Level it up by winning.

Keep a daily streak, join the weekly Reef Rally, and multiply your gems up to x5 after a win.

FAQ

How do I make an Ocean Gem in Turtle Bay?

Match 5 tiles of the same colour in a straight line.

What is the best combo?

Swapping two power-ups together. An Ocean Gem combined with another power-up clears the most tiles.

Is Turtle Bay the same game as Reef Rescue?

Yes. Reef Rescue was renamed to Turtle Bay and your progress is kept.
