Garden Flow guide: how to solve water pipe puzzles

Turn the pipes, connect the well to every thirsty flower and watch the garden bloom. These steps solve almost every board.

The rules in one minute

Tap a pipe to turn it. Your goal is to carry water from the well to every flower on the board. When every flower gets water, the level is solved, your plant grows and a rain shower fills the garden.

A method that works on every board

Start at the edges and corners. A pipe in a corner can only face a few ways, so it is often already decided.

Fix the obvious pieces first. Dead ends next to a flower and straight pipes between two walls usually have one correct direction.

Follow the water. Work outward from the well one pipe at a time instead of turning random pieces.

Watch for loops. If water reaches a pipe from two sides, one of them is wrong.

When to use a hint

Hints are there for the moment you are truly stuck. Try the edge method first, then use a hint on the pipe closest to the well, because one correct pipe there often unlocks the rest.

Daily gifts give free coins every day.

Flowers and pipe styles

A new flower joins your collection every 10 levels.

You can change the look of the pipes: Clay, Bamboo, Copper and Crystal.

The puzzles play offline, so Garden Flow is a good game for flights and commutes.

FAQ

How do I solve a hard Garden Flow level?

Start from the corners and edges, fix the pipes that can face only one way, then follow the water out from the well.

Does Garden Flow work offline?

Yes, the puzzles need no internet.

Is Garden Flow the same game as Water Garden?

Yes. Water Garden was renamed to Garden Flow and your progress is kept.
