Feather Strike guide: bosses, weapons and tips

Stack your power, pick the right weapon and take down the Bug Emperor. A quick guide to the galaxy shooter from Lagoon Isle.

Controls and power

Drag anywhere to move. Your bird fires by itself, so all your attention can go to dodging.

Every power orb adds more shots, up to power level 8. Grab orbs early in a stage, because a high power level makes the boss much shorter.

The three weapon pickups

Fan Shot spreads bullets wide. Best for swarms of small bugs.

Rapid Beam focuses damage straight ahead. Best for bosses.

Storm Feathers change your fire pattern again for mixed waves.

Switch to the beam just before a boss appears if you can.

Worlds and bosses

There are 5 worlds and 50 stages, each world with a mini boss and a giant boss:

Meadow Skies: Queen Hornet. Sunset Dunes: Scorpion King. Storm Clouds: Spider Matriarch. Moon Orbit: the Hive Mothership. Hive Galaxy: The Bug Emperor.

Survival tips

Stay low on the screen. You get more time to react to enemy fire.

Move in small steps. Big swipes put you into bullets you did not see.

Save a bomb for boss attack patterns that fill the screen. A bomb clears everything at once.

Upgrade one favourite bird first. There are 9 birds, from the Chick to the Phoenix, each with its own weapon.

Stars, endless mode and rewards

Every stage has 3 stars to earn. Replay early stages with a stronger bird to collect missing stars.

Endless mode asks one question: how many waves can you survive?

Daily missions, a daily gift, the lucky nest and flight chests give coins for upgrades. Sign in with Google to keep your birds in the cloud.

FAQ

Who is the final boss in Feather Strike?

The Bug Emperor, in the Hive Galaxy.

How many stages are there?

50 stages in 5 worlds, plus an endless mode.

Is Feather Strike the same game as Feather Flight?

Yes. Feather Flight was renamed to Feather Strike and your coins and progress are kept.
