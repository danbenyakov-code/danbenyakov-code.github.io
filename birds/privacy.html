Feather Flight: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026. This policy covers the Android game Feather Flight: Sky Birds by Lagoon Isle (package com.lagoonisle.feather).

Optional Lagoon Isle account (Google sign-in)

You can play as a guest without signing up; then your progress stays only on your device. If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and a user ID from Google (via Firebase Authentication) and store your game progress in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), so you can keep playing on any device. The same Lagoon Isle account also records which of our games you play (Lagoon Isle, Reef Rescue, Water Garden, Feather Flight, Coin Beach, Tiny Tide Heroes) to give you one-time bonuses. We do not collect your contacts or files.

Deleting your account

In the game open Settings, then Lagoon Isle account, then Delete account. Your account and cloud progress are deleted immediately. You can also email us; see how to delete your account.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), approximate location based on IP address, app interactions and diagnostic data to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time. See how Google uses information from apps. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

In-app purchases (Google Play and RevenueCat)

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We use RevenueCat to validate purchases; it receives an anonymous app user ID and the purchase record. We never see your payment details.

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played and to improve it. It collects an app instance ID, in-game events (such as sessions, progress, sharing and rating prompts), device model, Android version, app version, language and approximate location (country or region, based on IP address). We do not send your name or email address to Analytics. See Firebase privacy information.

Ratings and sharing

The game may ask you to rate it through the Google Play in-app review feature; your rating goes directly to Google Play. If you choose to share, the game opens your device's share menu and nothing is sent unless you pick an app and send it yourself.

Your rights (EEA, UK and Switzerland: GDPR)

Controller: Lagoon Isle, contact info.naimly@gmail.com.

Legal bases: we process account, cloud save and purchase data to provide the game you asked for (performance of a contract); analytics, security and fraud prevention data on the basis of our legitimate interest in running and improving the game; and personalised advertising only with your consent, which you give or refuse in the consent message shown on first launch.

Your rights: you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, restrict or object to its processing, and withdraw your consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. Email us and we will answer within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

International transfers: our service providers (Google and RevenueCat) may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Retention: cloud data is kept until you delete your account; analytics data is kept according to Google Analytics retention settings; data on your device is removed when you uninstall the game.

We do not sell personal data.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children.

Data retention and deletion

Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes all progress stored on your device. For any request about data held by our partners, contact us and we will help within 30 days.

Security

All data our partners receive is sent encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
