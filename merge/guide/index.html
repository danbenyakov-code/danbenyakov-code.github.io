Lagoon Isle guide: merge tips and events

Help Lily rebuild her grandmother's island. These tips keep your board tidy, your energy full and your islands growing.

The basics

Merge matching items to make a better item. Generators create new items, island residents ask for items in orders, and every finished order helps you build.

Buildings upgrade in three stages, and you sail between eight tropical islands.

Keep your board clear

A full board is the most common reason players get stuck. Merge before you tap a generator again.

Focus on what the current orders ask for. Items nobody needs yet can go to storage.

There are more than 10 merge chains and over 100 items, so plan which chains you are working on.

Save energy

Breezy Palms drop free items with no energy. Tap them whenever they are ready.

Daily tasks, reward chests and a daily gift that grows every day you return all help.

Events

Harbor Rush: deliver orders and pick 1 of 3 prizes.

Up to 5 short events run at once in the Events hub, including the Tide Pool board, Gem Hunt, Fog Voyage and Color Code.

Join an event when its board matches the chains you are already merging. It doubles the value of the same moves.

Album and collections

The card album has 12 sets, and spare cards go to the Star Shop.

Your progress is saved in the cloud, so you can switch phones.

FAQ

How do I get more energy in Lagoon Isle?

Use Breezy Palms, which drop free items with no energy, and collect daily tasks, reward chests and the daily gift.

How many islands are there?

Eight, each with its own chapter of the story.

Can I move my progress to a new phone?

Yes. Progress is saved in the cloud.
