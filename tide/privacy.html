Tiny Tide Heroes: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026. This policy covers the Android game Tiny Tide Heroes by Lagoon Isle (package com.lagoonisle.tidehero).

Nickname and public hero profile

When you start the game you choose a nickname. To keep nicknames unique, the game creates an anonymous user ID through Google Firebase Authentication and stores your nickname in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). Your nickname, hero level, class and appearance are shown to other players (for example in towns and on the weekly world boss leaderboard, together with your damage score). Do not use your real name or personal information as a nickname.

Optional Lagoon Isle account (Google sign-in)

You can play without signing in; then your progress stays only on your device. If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and a user ID from Google (via Firebase Authentication) and store your game progress in Cloud Firestore, so you can keep playing on any device. The same Lagoon Isle account also records which of our games you play (Lagoon Isle, Reef Rescue, Water Garden, Feather Flight, Coin Beach, Tiny Tide Heroes) to give you one-time bonuses. We do not collect your contacts or files.

Deleting your account and data

In the game open Settings, then Lagoon Isle account, then Delete account. Your account, cloud progress, nickname, public hero profile and leaderboard entry are deleted immediately. You can also email us; see how to delete your account.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob, mostly rewarded ads that you choose to watch. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), approximate location based on IP address, app interactions and diagnostic data to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time. See how Google uses information from apps. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played and to improve it. It collects an app instance ID, in-game events (such as sessions, progress, boss wins, sharing and rating prompts), device model, Android version, app version, language and approximate location (country or region, based on IP address). We do not send your name, email address or nickname to Analytics. See Firebase privacy information.

Ratings and sharing

The game may ask you to rate it through the Google Play in-app review feature; your rating and review go directly to Google Play and we do not receive them inside the game. If you choose to share, the game opens your device's share menu and nothing is sent unless you pick an app and send it yourself.

In-app purchases (Google Play and RevenueCat)

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We use RevenueCat to validate purchases; it receives an anonymous app user ID and the purchase record. We never see your payment details. Paid random rewards (such as lanterns) show their odds in the game.

Feedback and support form

From the game settings (and occasionally after a good moment in the game) you can send us a message. When you press Send, we receive: the text you wrote, the message type you chose (suggestion, problem, support or other), the email address you typed (optional, only if you want a reply), the game name and version, your phone model and Android version, and how many times you opened the game. The message is delivered to our support inbox through Web3Forms, a form-to-email service. We use it only to answer you and to improve the game, we never sell it, and you can ask us to delete it at any time by writing to info.naimly@gmail.com. If sending fails, the game can open your own email app instead, and nothing is sent until you press send there.

Your rights (EEA, UK and Switzerland: GDPR)

Controller: Lagoon Isle, contact info.naimly@gmail.com.

Legal bases: we process account, cloud save and purchase data to provide the game you asked for (performance of a contract); analytics, security and fraud prevention data on the basis of our legitimate interest in running and improving the game; and personalised advertising only with your consent, which you give or refuse in the consent message shown on first launch.

Your rights: you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, restrict or object to its processing, and withdraw your consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. Email us and we will answer within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

International transfers: our service providers (Google and RevenueCat) may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Retention: cloud data is kept until you delete your account; analytics data is kept according to Google Analytics retention settings; data on your device is removed when you uninstall the game.

We do not sell personal data.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children.

Data retention

Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes all progress stored on your device. Cloud data is kept until you delete your account. For any request about data held by our partners, contact us and we will help within 30 days.

Security

All data our partners receive is sent encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
