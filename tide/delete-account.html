Delete your Tiny Tide Heroes account

App: Tiny Tide Heroes (Lagoon Isle). This page explains how to delete your account, your nickname and the data linked to them.

Option 1: in the game (instant)

  1. Open Tiny Tide Heroes and tap the Settings button (gear, top corner).
  2. Under Lagoon Isle account, tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and your cloud-saved progress are deleted immediately.

Option 2: by email

Email info.naimly@gmail.com with the subject "Tiny Tide Heroes account deletion" from the Google address you used to sign in, or include your nickname if you never signed in. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (user ID, email address and name).
  • Your cloud-saved game progress (hero, gear, gold, gems, level, quests).
  • Your nickname, public hero profile and weekly leaderboard entry.

What is kept

  • Purchase records that Google Play and RevenueCat must keep for legal, tax and fraud-prevention reasons, under their own retention policies.
  • Progress stored only on your device stays there until you uninstall the game or clear its data.

See also our privacy policy.
