Tide Ronin guide: classes, bosses and leveling

Pick a class, build your gear and beat the legendary sea bosses of the Lagoon Isles. A practical guide for new heroes.

Which class should I pick?

Ronin: a tough sword master with lifesteal. The easiest class to learn, because it heals while it fights.

Miko: a storm shrine maiden who hits many foes at once. Great for clearing maps fast.

Archer: long range critical hits. The boss hunter.

Ninja: fast poison and shadow strikes for players who like speed.

There is no wrong pick. If you are new to action RPGs, start with the Ronin.

Leveling fast

Every map shows its recommended power. Farm on the highest map your power allows: it gives the best rewards for your time.

Learn and level your skills, then set up your two skill bars.

Switch on AUTO when you are farming and collect offline rewards when you come back.

You can level up to 80, and your third class advancement opens on the way.

Gear and enhancement

There are 8 gear slots: weapon, helm, armor, cape, belt, boots, accessory and ring.

Gear can be enhanced up to +15. Use charms to protect it on risky steps.

Pets follow you and collect loot, so you never miss a drop.

Beating the bosses

The sea bosses are Captain Clamshell, the Kelp Hydra, the Angler Queen, the giant Umibozu, the Yuki Hydra and the Raijin Colossus.

Read the warning before each big attack and dodge at that moment, not after.

Fill the Tide gauge during the fight and awaken for more damage, more speed and faster skills when the boss is open.

A weekly world boss has a global damage leaderboard.

Towns and maps

Six towns and 30 maps, from sunny shores and kelp forests to ember reefs, the frozen Frostveil Fjord and the Raijin Peaks. Fill the monster book to earn stars for every species.

FAQ

What is the best class in Tide Ronin for beginners?

The Ronin. Its lifesteal keeps you alive while you learn the game.

What is the max level?

Level 80.

Is Tide Ronin the same game as Tiny Tide Heroes?

Yes. Tiny Tide Heroes was renamed to Tide Ronin and your hero and progress are kept.
