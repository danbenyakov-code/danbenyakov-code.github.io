Coin Beach: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026. This policy covers the Android game Coin Beach: Coin Pusher by Lagoon Isle (package com.lagoonisle.coinbeach).

Optional Lagoon Isle account (Google sign-in)

You can play as a guest without signing up; then your progress stays only on your device. If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and a user ID from Google (via Firebase Authentication) and store your game progress and sea friend album in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), so you can keep playing on any device. The same Lagoon Isle account also records which of our games you play (Lagoon Isle, Reef Rescue, Water Garden, Feather Flight, Coin Beach) to give you one-time bonuses. We do not collect your contacts or files.

Deleting your account

In the game open Settings, then Lagoon Isle account, then Delete account. Your account and cloud progress are deleted immediately. You can also email us; see how to delete your account.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), approximate location based on IP address, app interactions and diagnostic data to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time. See how Google uses information from apps. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

In-app purchases (Google Play and RevenueCat)

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We use RevenueCat to validate purchases; it receives an anonymous app user ID and the purchase record. We never see your payment details.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children.

Data retention and deletion

Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes all progress stored on your device. For any request about data held by our partners, contact us and we will help within 30 days.

Security

All data our partners receive is sent encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
