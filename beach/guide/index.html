How to play

Tap to drop a coin, hold to drop more. The pusher shoves the pile forward and every coin that falls over the edge is yours.

Free coins refill over time, so there is always a reason to come back.

Where to drop

Drop where the pile is already close to the edge. A coin that lands on an overhanging stack pushes more coins over than one that lands on the bare floor.

Aim through the glowing ring to light the bulbs. When all bulbs are lit, the bonus reel spins.

Holding to drop many coins at once is best right before the pusher moves forward.

Power-ups

Tidal Wave sweeps the whole beach forward. Use it when the front of the board is packed.

Safety Walls close the side gaps so fewer coins are lost.

Coin Shower drops a rain of coins across the board.

Mystery eggs and the album

Push a mystery egg over the edge to hatch a sea friend.

There are 40 sea friends to collect: common, rare, epic and legendary, from the Crab to the Treasure Dragon.

Level up for gems and eggs, and finish the daily missions and daily gift.

The five beaches

Sunny Shore, Coral Reef, Jungle Lagoon, Ice Bay and Treasure Isle.

Coin Beach is just for fun: no real money can be won and coins have no cash value.
